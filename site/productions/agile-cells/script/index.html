Agile Cells: the script

The film as a screenplay: what is seen, what is said and what is written on screen, scene by scene. Version v0.6, 8 scenes, 81 seconds, 213 words of voice.

Voice: Mistral voxtral-mini-tts-3, made by AI and labelled on screen in every frame. The picture directions are the storyboard’s.

1. THE PROPOSITION 0:00 to 0:07, scene component cells-intro

VOICE

Agile is great. But it struggles to scale. Now we have a way to make it scale: Agile Cells.

ON SCREEN

A small team strains as it grows, then splits into cells. Agile Cells.

2. FOUR VALUES 0:07 to 0:15, scene component snowbird-page

The one page of the Manifesto, its four lines lit in turn: the left half bright, 'over' in amber, the right half dim.

Reused: lab/snowbird-page, span 9.6 to 14 s, as in The story of Agile. The four values quoted exactly from agilemanifesto.org.

VOICE

In 2001, seventeen people wrote four values. The first: Individuals and interactions over processes and tools.

ON SCREEN

Individuals and interactions over processes and tools; Working software over comprehensive documentation; Customer collaboration over contract negotiation; Responding to change over following a plan

3. THE VALUES ARE SOUND 0:15 to 0:31, scene component cells-sound

VOICE

As one of its authors, Ron Jeffries, wrote in 2018: the values and principles of the Manifesto still offer the best way I know to build software. The values are sound. What hardens, as it grows, is the word, and the way it is run.

ON SCREEN

“the values and principles of the Manifesto for Agile Software Development still offer the best way I know to build software” (Ron Jeffries, 2018). Four warm lights inside a hardening shell.

4. WHAT A CELL IS 0:31 to 0:44, scene component cells-grow

Inside the membrane: one warm light (a person) and a few cooler ones (agents). Three cards slide out of the cell, labelled roles, rules, work.

The cell forms, the members join one by one, the files fan out beside it.

VOICE

Agile Cells keeps the values, and scales them. A cell is a small team of people and agents, formed for one piece of work and ended after it. Its roles, rules and work are files anyone can read.

ON SCREEN

roles, rules, work

5. TEN ROLES, OR FIFTY 0:44 to 0:53, scene component cells-grow

The cell grows in steps, 10, 20, 30, 50, a counter beside it; inside, the lights arrange themselves into an org chart with branches.

Each step lands on a word of the voice. Ends on the full chart (the same frame the hook previewed).

VOICE

A cell can have ten roles, or twenty, thirty, fifty. Almost a mini company, with its own org chart, for one task.

ON SCREEN

10, 20, 30, 50 roles

6. WHAT A CELL MADE 0:53 to 1:03, scene component refresh-trailer

VOICE

And here is Agile working. One cell made a real film, How we refresh a session, version after version, in one day.

ON SCREEN

How we refresh a session, as live cards of its scenes.

7. WE DID IT 1:03 to 1:12, scene component refresh-cell

The real chart of the cell that made How we refresh a session, growing from Dinis Cruz down to the helpers, drawn round as a cell, then archived to a shelf with its files.

Reused: lab/refresh-cell, span 0 to 14 s.

VOICE

One person, a lead agent, scene builders, a critic and test viewers. Set up for that one film, and taken down when it is done.

ON SCREEN

Dinis Cruz; account C: c.maestro, conductor.c, c.productions with 5 scene-smiths, a critic, 5 test viewers and 2 helper sessions, and design.c, higgsfield.c, accountant.c; account B: b.maestro, higgsfield.b, elevenlabs.b. An agile cell. Archived: its files stay.

8. AGILE, AT ANY SIZE 1:12 to 1:21, scene component cells-close

VOICE

So Agile can scale: a cell for each task, built when it is needed, closed when it is done, its files kept. That is Agile Cells.

ON SCREEN

Cells form, work and fold onto shelves of files. Agile Cells. sovereignwebsite.org
