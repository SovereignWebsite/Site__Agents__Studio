Agile Cells v0.5: the storyboard

Every scene in order, with three frames, its words and its length. Press a frame to see that moment in the preview; play one scene; mark it and comment.

In review Version v0.5 follows Dinis Cruz's feedback on v0.3 and v0.4. Nobody has signed it. Nothing is published anywhere. agilecells.org did not answer on 28 November 2026; what the film says about a cell comes from the seed pages in the AgileCells and AgentiCCells repositories. From Dinis Cruz's feedback on v0.3 and v0.4: an opening that states the proposition before any explanation (Martin Fowler's 2018 words on what Agile became, quoted exactly and credited, then lab/cells-intro: a team strains as it grows and splits into cells, 'Agile Cells: the early values, at scale', and an agenda of what is coming); the proof as the punchline ('here is Agile working'); a closing that consolidates (lab/cells-close: cells built, working and folded onto shelves of files under Agile Cells and sovereignwebsite.org). 74 s. Already fixed in v0.4 before his note: the vertical cells out, the web address on the end card.
