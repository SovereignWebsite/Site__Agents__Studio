Agile Cells: the reviews

Everything anyone, or anything, has said about this film: test viewers, critics, and signed reviews. Newest version first.

The owner’s feedback

“it's okay for the manifest ... on Agile. That's fine. Because this is going for people who know about it. So leave the vertical cells. We'll add that later. And so the web address for now, make it sovereignweb.org. Yes, the AI [label at the end] is good.”

Dinis Cruz, in c.productions's session (speech to text, relayed), 2026-11-28, on v0.3. Done in v0.4 (the address as sovereignwebsite.org: sovereignweb.org showed only an error page that day).

“spectacular ... a great explanation of it. I think what's missing is just the intro and the end ... Why are we doing this? What are we talking about? What's the point of the cells? ... this is the first time people are going to be hearing Agile Cells, and we should immediately put that proposition: we think Agile is a great framework, it just struggles to scale, and now we can, and here is an example of us using Agile Cells for real. Almost that should be the punchline: here is Agile Cells working. Here is Agile working ... The last slide just needs that wrap-up consolidation ... and a couple of little text overflows.”

Dinis Cruz, in c.productions's session (speech to text, relayed), 2026-11-28, on v0.3 and v0.4. Done in v0.5.

“the target audience is people who run Agile ... people who are already committed to the Agile methodology, and they should have empathy with the whole thing of, it's like the other guy says: look, the principles are sound. It's just that the word Agile, but also the execution, becomes quite solidified. And that's kind of what we're saying here. So the message is: Agile is great, but it struggles to scale. That was my words. So now we have a way to make it scale. And I think that's super powerful.”

Dinis Cruz, in c.productions's session (speech to text, relayed), 2026-11-28, on v0.5. Done in v0.6.

Version v0.6

2026-11-28. From Dinis Cruz's feedback on v0.5: speaks to people who already run Agile, with empathy. Opens on the team's proposition, 'Agile is great. But it struggles to scale. Now we have a way to make it scale: Agile Cells' (the Fowler line is gone); the four values; Ron Jeffries's 2018 words that the values still offer the best way he knows, exactly quoted and credited, over a new scene (lab/cells-sound: the values stay lit while a hard shell grows round them), 'what hardens, as it grows, is the word, and the way it is run'; then the cell, the scale, the real film and the close. 82 s.
