Agile Cells / version v0.3

Agile Cells: v0.3

What an agile cell is and why Agile can scale and keep its first four values: a vertical film of about 82 seconds, drawn with WebGL in your browser, ending on the real cell of people and agents that made How we refresh a session, with a voice made by AI.

In review Version v0.6 follows Dinis Cruz's feedback on v0.5. Nobody has signed it. Nothing is published anywhere. agilecells.org did not answer on 28 November 2026; what the film says about a cell comes from the seed pages in the AgileCells and AgentiCCells repositories.

    Scene 1

    Your browser turns your voice into text (in Safari, Apple’s dictation). Some browsers send the sound to their maker to do it. This site receives no sound.

    Keys, with the player in focus: Space or K plays and pauses, the left and right arrows change scene, Home goes back to the start. With reduced motion set on your device, scenes change without movement. “Capture a file” plays the production from the start and records the picture and the voice in your browser, for a platform that needs a file. The voice is made by AI: Mistral voxtral-mini-tts-3, voice_3150 (James, a stock preset), played 1.25 times faster. It is no real person’s voice.
