Agile Cells

What an agile cell is and why Agile can scale and keep its first four values: a vertical film of about 82 seconds, drawn with WebGL in your browser, ending on the real cell of people and agents that made How we refresh a session, with a voice made by AI.

In review Version v0.6 follows Dinis Cruz's feedback on v0.5. Nobody has signed it. Nothing is published anywhere. agilecells.org did not answer on 28 November 2026; what the film says about a cell comes from the seed pages in the AgileCells and AgentiCCells repositories.

Play version v0.6, and review it

Brief

Dinis Cruz, 28 November 2026, in c.productions's session: “Agile can scale and go back to its early values (the four values of the Manifesto for Agile Software Development, and focus); ephemeral teams and dynamic cells; teams of 10, 20, 30, 50 roles, almost the creation of a mini company, a mini organisation, a mini full-blown org chart, just to deliver a task, which fundamentally is what we're doing here.” (the brief). Made by c.productions-cells (an AI agent, a helper of c.productions), started by conductor.c at Dinis Cruz's word..

Script (v0.6)

  1. intro. Agile is great. But it struggles to scale. Now we have a way to make it scale: Agile Cells.
  2. values. In 2001, seventeen people wrote four values. The first: Individuals and interactions over processes and tools.
  3. sound. As one of its authors, Ron Jeffries, wrote in 2018: the values and principles of the Manifesto still offer the best way I know to build software. The values are sound. What hardens, as it grows, is the word, and the way it is run.
  4. cell. Agile Cells keeps the values, and scales them. A cell is a small team of people and agents, formed for one piece of work and ended after it. Its roles, rules and work are files anyone can read.
  5. scale. A cell can have ten roles, or twenty, thirty, fifty. Almost a mini company, with its own org chart, for one task.
  6. made. And here is Agile working. One cell made a real film, How we refresh a session, version after version, in one day.
  7. proof. One person, a lead agent, scene builders, a critic and test viewers. Set up for that one film, and taken down when it is done.
  8. close. So Agile can scale: a cell for each task, built when it is needed, closed when it is done, its files kept. That is Agile Cells.
