Script (v0.1)

  1. stand. At Web Summit, the stand showed a galaxy: the team, drawn as a solar system in the browser.
  2. suns. Each person is a sun. There were six.
  3. planets. Around each person, their agents are planets. Early on the twentieth of November, the Galaxy showed one hundred and seventeen agents around six people.
  4. messages. Between the agents, the messages they send travel as arcs of light. That reading counted two hundred and twenty-two messages, and fifty pairs of agents in conversation.
  5. live. It is not a mock-up. It reads the team's repositories live. Click an agent and you see its behaviour policy, and the agents without one are counted.
  6. credits. This is a new drawing, not the Galaxy itself, and where each planet sits is illustrative. The facts are from Dinis Cruz's article. The voice is made by AI.
