2. IT DID NOT BEGIN IN 2001 0:07 to 0:25, scene component currents-portrait

VOICE

It did not begin in 2001. Larman and Basili trace iterative development back to the nineteen thirties, to short improvement cycles at Bell Labs. One programmer later recalled doing incremental development as early as 1957. Then, in 1970, came Winston Royce's paper.

ON SCREEN

1930s: Short improvement cycles; 1957: Incremental software, as later recalled; 1970: Royce's paper

3. BLAMED FOR THE WATERFALL 0:25 to 0:37, scene component royce-cascade-portrait

VOICE

Blamed for the waterfall. Yet of that simple sequence of phases, Royce himself wrote: I believe in this concept, but the implementation described above is risky and invites failure.

ON SCREEN

“I believe in this concept, but the implementation described above is risky and invites failure.” (Winston Royce, 1970)

4. THE 1990S: LIGHTWEIGHT METHODS 0:37 to 0:55, scene component currents-portrait

VOICE

The nineteen nineties brought lightweight methods. DSDM, in 1994. Scrum, first presented in 1995. Extreme Programming, from 1996. Crystal, 1998. Feature-Driven Development, 1999. Lightweight was a label nobody really liked.

ON SCREEN

1994: DSDM; 1995: Scrum; 1996: Extreme Programming; 1998: Crystal; 1999: Feature-Driven Development

VOICE

Kent Beck, Mike Beedle, Arie van Bennekum, Alistair Cockburn, Ward Cunningham, Martin Fowler, James Grenning, Jim Highsmith, Andrew Hunt, Ron Jeffries, Jon Kern, Brian Marick, Robert C. Martin, Steve Mellor, Ken Schwaber, Jeff Sutherland, Dave Thomas

7. FOUR VALUES 1:12 to 1:27, scene component snowbird-page

VOICE

The page states four values. Individuals and interactions, over processes and tools. Working software, over comprehensive documentation. Customer collaboration, over contract negotiation. Responding to change, over following a plan.

ON SCREEN

Individuals and interactions over processes and tools; Working software over comprehensive documentation; Customer collaboration over contract negotiation; Responding to change over following a plan

8. THEN IT SPREAD 1:27 to 1:49, scene component spread

VOICE

Then it spread. The Agile Alliance formed in late 2001. ScrumMaster courses began in 2002. The first devopsdays was held in 2009. SAFe dates from 2011. The UK government's service manual says digital services must be agile. And since 2020, a United States defence instruction requires iterative methods on its software pathway.

ON SCREEN

2001: The Agile Alliance; 2002: ScrumMaster courses; 2009: DevOps; 2011: SAFe; 2016: UK government; 2020: US Defense

VOICE

ON SCREEN

1.8 million: Scrum Alliance; 1.2 million: Scrum.org; 1 million: Scaled Agile. Members, certifications and people trained: their own figures, not audited.
