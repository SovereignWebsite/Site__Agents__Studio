  1. 1 The number hook, 0:00 to 0:07, 8 s, lab scene refresh-scroll

    This agent wrote three quarters of a million tokens. To do it, it re-read ninety-five million.

    On screen: written: 745,255. re-read: 95,247,818.

  2. 2 A line per message, a curve in total why, 0:07 to 0:17, 10 s, lab scene refresh-scroll

    Every turn, a chat re-reads itself from the top. So each message costs a little more than the last. That is a straight line. Add the lines up, and you get a curve.

    On screen: one message: a line. all of them: a curve.

  3. 3 The line nobody stops at moment, 0:17 to 0:27, 9 s, lab scene refresh-line

    The rule says: hand over before three hundred thousand tokens. Every hand-over we can evidence came later. The latest, at eight hundred and forty-five thousand.

    On screen: hand over before 300,000. 484,000. 845,000.

  4. 4 What the old session writes writes, 0:27 to 0:39, 12 s, lab scene refresh-shelf

    So the old session writes down what the next one needs. What is open. What it learnt. What to do first. A few pages, in the repository. Nothing may live only in the session.

    On screen: role, now, restart.

  5. 5 What the new one reads reads, 0:39 to 0:48, 9 s, lab scene refresh-shelf

    A person starts a new session with one line. It reads the rules, its role, its state. It never opens the old transcript.

    On screen: Read restart.md and continue.

  6. 6 What is lost, and what carries keys, 0:48 to 1:02, 14 s, lab scene refresh-keys

    Some things are lost on purpose. The old key is retired; the new session cuts its own. Secrets sealed to the old key are sealed again, and checked to open.

    On screen: retired.

  7. 7 Did it work test, 1:02 to 1:09, 7 s, lab scene refresh-keys

    The test: does the fresh session reach the same next step as the old one? If not, a file is missing. Fix the file.

    On screen: what is next.

  8. 8 The saving saving, 1:09 to 1:24, 14 s, lab scene refresh-saving

    One measured hand-over. Before: eight hundred and sixty thousand tokens of context. After: one hundred and nineteen thousand. Eighty-six per cent less to re-read, on every turn. About seventy-five thousand is a floor that stays.

    On screen: 859,600. 118,600. 86% less. floor: about 75,000.

  9. 9 The lesson lesson, 1:24 to 1:28, 5 s, lab scene refresh-saving

    Write it early. Keep state in files. Load only what you need.

    On screen: Write it early. Keep state in files. Load only what you need.
