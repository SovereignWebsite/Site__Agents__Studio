How we refresh a session v0.4: the storyboard

Every scene in order, with three frames, its words and its length. Press a frame to see that moment in the preview; play one scene; mark it and comment.

In review Version v0.8 ends on the agile cell and 'This is how we scale.' Nobody has signed it. Nothing is published anywhere. From Dinis Cruz's second feedback and five synthetic test viewers of v0.3, who all asked for the practical thing: a line of context on every scene that glues the pictures into steps (the problem, why, our rule, step 1, step 2, step 3, the result, copy this); the voice now says what we do as three steps; the last scene is the team that made the film, with its models, in place of flat credits; the voice no longer says that it is made by AI (the label on every frame does).
