How we refresh a session v0.2: the storyboard

Every scene in order, with three frames, its words and its length. Press a frame to see that moment in the preview; play one scene; mark it and comment.

In review Version v0.8 ends on the agile cell and 'This is how we scale.' Nobody has signed it. Nothing is published anywhere. Second pass, after two critics judged v0.1 (a critic sub-agent and an outside model through OpenRouter; their notes are in v0.1/notes/critics.json). The keys scene is cut to keep one story; what is lost at a hand-over stays on the document page. Narration trimmed: about 76 seconds, nine scenes. Every lab scene had one more round: the first frame is no longer empty, 'tokens' is on screen, the 300,000 line is a bar that gets broken, the new light is larger and passes the rolled scroll, the test starts clean, '86% less' is the biggest thing in the film, and the lesson shows one phrase at a time. Still no music or effects.
